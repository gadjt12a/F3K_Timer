Covers fw-v31 through fw-v37 — the last packaged release was fw-v30, so this one
carries seven builds' worth of change. Sessions 66 and 67.

FLASHING
========

From Linux, use the script that ships beside this directory — it handles the OTA
slot trap, the missing stub flasher, and the serial logger holding the port:

  sudo ../../flash-timer.sh fw-v37

Or by hand:

  0x00000  bootloader.bin
  0x08000  partitions.bin
  0x10000  firmware.bin

  esptool --chip esp32s3 --port /dev/ttyACM0 --no-stub \
      write_flash 0x0 bootloader.bin 0x8000 partitions.bin 0x10000 firmware.bin

⚠ THEN CLEAR otadata, or the timer may boot the firmware you just replaced:

  head -c 8192 /dev/zero | tr '\0' '\377' > blank.bin
  esptool --chip esp32s3 --port /dev/ttyACM0 --no-stub write_flash 0xe000 blank.bin

  A timer that has taken an over-the-air update is running from ota_1, so a wired
  write to 0x10000 fills the slot that is NOT running. esptool reports "Hash of
  data verified", the timer reboots, and comes up on the OLD build. This has cost
  time in three separate sessions. Never trust the flash succeeding — confirm the
  version on the timer's screen or from its JOIN line at the base.

BASE STATION PAIRING
====================

⚠ REQUIRES a base station from 2026-08-02 or later (commit bf976c0 or newer).
This is a hard pairing, not a nice-to-have:

  - The timer expects `TASK … task= mode=` to know it is flying Poker or a Ladder.
    An older base sends no mode= and every task runs plain, so no target picker is
    ever offered and Poker cannot be flown at all.
  - The timer sends `FLIGHT … target=`. An older base ignores it, and Poker is
    then scored on the FLOWN time instead of the announced one — wrong scores,
    silently.
  - `WTSYNC t=` and `OTAPUSH` are both new. An older base sends neither: a
    mid-working reconnect gets the wrong clock, and firmware will not push.

WHAT'S NEW SINCE fw-v30
=======================

Poker and Ladder target tasks, then eleven defects found by pressing the buttons:

  - Poker: declare a target before the launch, immediately after it (F3K.11.5
    permits this), or during prep. Missed calls stay armed and are re-flown; W
    gets one attempt only. Scores the ANNOUNCED time, never the flown one.
  - W resolves at the LAUNCH, not the call. Resolving at the call made a W
    unflyable unless you threw in the same second — a pilot who flew the whole
    window scored zero.
  - Ladders (D, K, M) advance only when the rung is reached; a miss is re-flown
    against the same time. K and M take an explicit rungs= list.
  - The flight clock counts DOWN to the target and turns orange past it.
  - Time Up shows which calls stood, in green, with their launch numbers —
    a best-times ranking is meaningless when only achieved calls score.
  - Up to 20 flights (was 10 — past ten they were silently dropped), paged with L.
  - FLYING (3) — the launch number on the running screen.
  - Connected to a base, the settings chain is closed: R-hold goes to Round Recall.
    Working time and discipline belong to the base.
  - Firmware is base-managed: the base pushes updates to any connected timer on an
    older build, when no heat is loaded and no round is running.
  - The screen no longer blanks mid-flight while connected, cable or not.
  - Jumped starts and scratches both count as a launch and score zero.

⚠ A timer on fw-v36 or older cannot be updated over the air by the base: OTAPUSH
did not exist before fw-v37, so it ignores the push. Those need this cable flash
once, and will auto-update from then on.
